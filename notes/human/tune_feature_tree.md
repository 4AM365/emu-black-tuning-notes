# Tune feature tree

> Digest of [tune_feature_tree.md](../tune_feature_tree.md) — the dense note is canonical; if they disagree, it wins.

Catalog of every tunable symbol in the Supra tune — 1771 symbols (428 tables, 1343 scalars) — filed under the EMU Black software's own 23 top-level pages.
Each page section lists its tables (with dimensions) and scalars, and the page index links to that feature's notes page.
Open it to find where a symbol lives in the software, check a table's size, or jump to the notes page covering a feature.
