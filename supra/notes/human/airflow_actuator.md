# Airflow / idle DBW calibration reference

> Digest of [airflow_actuator.md](../airflow_actuator.md) — the dense note is canonical; if they disagree, it wins.

**What this covers:** the live state of the idle DBW system — actuator range, where the throttle actually stops and why, airflow tables, PID gains, measured loop stability — and the rescale rules to reapply whenever the range changes.

**The rules:**

- Actuator range is **[1.5%, 6.5%] TPS** (width 5.0) since 2026-09-19 evening: `TPS% = 1.5 + airflow%/100 × 5.0`; `dbwMinDC` is −100. Everything below about floors, stuck plates and the +1.1 frame shift was measured on a **fouled throttle body** — see [tb_clean_2026-09-19](tb_clean_2026-09-19.md). Was [2.4, 8.0] (05-22 → 09-15), confirmed live on the 09-08 log.
- **The plate stops at ~4.8% TPS because `dbwMinDC` = −35% stops it there — that is a tune value, not a mechanical end-stop.** Raise the clamp and it goes lower. Below ~6% the spring force knees up steeply, so each extra unit of duty buys less closure.
- **No constant floor is an anti-stall safeguard — in duty or in position.** The sustaining position moves with friction: at the *same* 96 °C coolant, cold oil needs **51–58 airflow %** and hot oil **28–31** (oil dataset). That ~25-point swing is 4× the entire dead band below the floor (~6 airflow %). Set the floor for cold oil and the hot engine idles high with no authority; set it for hot oil and it guarantees nothing. Confirmed the hard way: two stalls with an under-supplied `idleArmedAirFlow`.
- **The only floor that tracks the condition is `idleAirPIDOutMin`**, because it is airflow % *relative to the Active-state table*. Absolute floors can only say "don't command somewhere useless". Caveat: a relative floor is only as good as the feedforward under it, and with the oil correction off, coolant can't see viscosity — so there is currently **no condition-following stall guarantee on this car**.
- Two floors, two scopes: `idleDBWTargetMin` bounds only the idle strategy; `dbwMinDC` bounds every source (idle, characteristic, cranking). On this car `dbwMinDC` is effectively an **idle-region** parameter — 70.4 of its 70.7 s on the rail are at TPS ≤ 10, and big closures touch the rail for a median 0.04 s — so moving it costs ~nothing in driveability.
- **`dbwMinDC` → floor:** 0 → 8.5–9 (limp-home rest), −3 → 5.8, −6 → 5.6, −33 → 5.2, −35 → 4.80. Violently nonlinear: 27 duty points buy 0.4 % TPS, then 2 points buy the next 0.4. Reaching a position costs more duty than holding it (approach to 4.7 needs −34; 5.6 holds on −3).
- **Duty is the wrong unit for a safeguard** — motor force ∝ duty × battery voltage, so 12.0 → 14.4 V moves the floor by more than the whole −33…−35 band, and cranking at ~10 V is the worst case. Use `dbwMinDC` for authority, `idleDBWTargetMin` for the guarantee.
- Raising `idleDBWTargetMin` to ~4.4 is free today (all unreachable travel) and makes each airflow-% 1.56× finer: rescale `new = 1.5556 × old − 55.56`, multiply additive corrections, gains and clamps by 1.5556. It is **not** the stall fix — 4.4 is still below the sustaining position.
- **The TPS frame moved ~+1.1% between 08-30 and 09-08.** Same RPM, same MAP, same base table — the position delivering it went 4.0 → 5.2. **Resolved 09-19: carbon fouling** — cleaning the TB took the hot position 5.9 → 2.5 in one step; the +1.1 was one step on the fouling curve. That shift, not the ramp offset, is what put the base airflow table on the money.
- **The 200 ramp-down offset works, and what still hurts is the decay rate.** All six ACTIVE entries on 09-08 hand over at the 1225 cap (base 1025 + 200). The two that arrive falling at −123/−137 rpm/s track the 100 rpm/s ramp to within 1–26 rpm and never wake the PID; the three arriving at −412…−528 outrun it by 96–116 rpm, push the PID to +8…+12 airflow % and +5…+6°, and rebound +26…+138 rpm. Arrival rate spans **4:1**, so no one decay rate can be in sync with all of them. Dropping 350 → 200 helped by moving the handover into a slower part of the decay (slowest arrival 207 → 123 rpm/s) and by halving the exposure window (3.5 s → 2.0 s). Zero ACTIVE→ARMED re-arms, no stall, worst dip 962.
- **Clutch filter (`goodlogwithclutch.csv`, same drive + the clutch channel):** the **clutch idle-up is not applying** — ARMED cap is 1225 and settled target 1025 whether the pedal is pressed or released. Confirmed in the XML: `idleClutchTrgtRPMIncrease` = 0 (`idleClutchEnablesClosedLoop` still 1). Clutch is never in during the ramp on the five genuine ramp-downs, so the offset verdict stands; the t=214.6 blip that produced the "+189 rpm / 11 rpm margin" figure was **clutch-in and is retired** — worst genuine excursion is +138, ~62 rpm of margin. Untested stress case: **clutch-in coastdown falls ~3× faster** (ARMED decel median −472 vs −161 rpm/s) and has never arrived at the cap in a scored event. A settled-PID difference by clutch state is **time drift, not the clutch** (PID walks +2.5 → −17.7 across the log as it heat-soaks).
- **09-08 config verified against `updatedarmedstate.xml.emub3`** — every log-derived number checks out: `idleRAMPDownOffset` 200, `idleRAMPDownDecayRate` 100, `idleDBWTargetMin/Max` 2.4/8.0, `dbwMinDC` −35, `idleRPM` 1025 flat from 90 °C, `idleActiveAirflow` 49.25 % at CLT 96 / target 1025 (= 5.158 % TPS). Airflow PID is now `idleAirFlowKP`/`KI` = 1024/1024 (KP 1.00, KI 1.00) vs 08-30's 307/410.
- **`idleActiveAirflow` is flat above 96 °C** — the CLT 96 and 105 columns are identical in every row, and `cltBins8` tops out at 105. Above 96 °C the open-loop feedforward is a constant by construction, so all heat-soak drift lands on the integrator. With the oil-pressure custom correction off, **nothing in the open loop can see heat soak once coolant is on the stat.**
- **The PID clamps are not what runs out** — `idleAirPIDOutMin` −25 and `idleAirFlowIntegralLimitMin` −25, and the log ends around −17 with 7–8 points to spare. The wall is `dbwMinDC`: commanding 4.20 % TPS with duty on the −35 rail and the plate parked at ~5.0.
- **Scheduled idle adders** — `idleACRPMIncrease`, `idleClutchTrgtRPMIncrease`, `idleDSGTorqueCorr` and **`idleCoolantFanCorr` (was 13)** are zero; **`idleAboveVSSTargetIncrease` was re-enabled at 200 / 5 km/h on 2026-09-19** (rolling entries arrive at 1425). The CLT-70 fan kick is an uncompensated load step.
- `idleMinMapToActivate` = 18 is **unbinding and correct** — zero samples of idle waiting on it; handovers fire on the RPM crossing at MAP 29–33. Valid window ~19–26; raising it re-creates the 2026-07 lockout stall.
- Encodings: ubyte airflow tables 0.5%/count; `idleDBWTargetMin/Max` 0.1/count; PID **gains** raw/1024, limits and clamps 1:1. `idleRAMPDownDecayRate` is 1 rpm/s per count. `idlePIDUpdateInterval` (=200) is inert.

**Key numbers (09-08 log, fouled TB — clean-TB numbers are in tb_clean_2026-09-19):** ramp offset **200 rpm**, decay **100 rpm/s**, ramp 2.0 s. Airflow PID ≈ **3–4× hotter** than the 08-30 XML. Return-to-idle overshoot **+202 → +50 rpm**, settle **7.8 s → 0.12 s**. Worst genuine (clutch-out) post-entry excursion +138 rpm, ~62 rpm under the cap; ~150 is the offset floor. Spring rest / limp-home ≈ **8.5–9% TPS**; holding 5.5% costs −32% duty, 4.5% is saturated.

**When to care:** any time you touch the actuator range, idle airflow tables, the airflow PID, the ramp-down offset, or `dbwMinDC`. Not covered by this log: anything cold; cold dense intake air (needs ~4.5–4.65%, *below* what the current clamp allows — expect a high idle on winter days). A/C at idle is impossible on this car (clutch gated at 1800 rpm), and `Idle airflow custom corr.` reads 0 because Will disabled the oil-pressure correction deliberately.
