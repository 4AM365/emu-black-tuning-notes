> Digest of [log_2026-09-29_allchannels_smoothclt.md](../log_2026-09-29_allchannels_smoothclt.md) — the dense note is canonical; if they disagree, it wins.

**What this covers:** one 21-minute cold-start-to-hot-idle log (E14): why RPM crashed after the start, why hot idle runs ~0.1 λ rich, and whether the CLT/IAT rewire worked.

**The rules:**

- **At the 500 threshold the start was never "designed".** The cranking tables got the engine to ~300 rpm, and the exit fired on one 586 pulse, switching fuel to VE × ASE. The engine then bogged at 455–586 rpm for 0.5 s with the starter still loaded. The idle PIDs engaged into the bog at 0.32 s (error −1146) and wound up. The engine pulled itself out 0.12–0.16 s after the plate moved (inside the ~750 ms air lag, so not the PID), and the wound-up PID drove the overshoot. Threshold now 750, afterstart delay 4.
- **The afterstart dip is a limit cycle, not a kP problem.** The airflow PID reads the ignition PID's *demand*; the afterstart lock/restore keeps the executed spark from following it for 2.8 s, so the slow air loop (~1 s lag) runs alone at full gain: flare to 1815, plunge to 1155 against ~1570.
- **Base airflow at CLT 26 was ~20 air-% short.** After the PID unwinds, the engine is on base alone; the integral sat at its ceiling for 30 s. Will's +20 base edit at 119 s confirms it.
- **The active-airflow table has been edited many times** (May rescales; whole-table rewrites 09-15 and twice 09-19; 4 cells 09-22). The 119 s edit raised the cold columns ~+16…+22, back near the pre-TB-clean 09-19 values; hot end untouched.
- **kP was 1.0 for the dip and halved at 480 s.** Replay says 0.5 would trim the swing ~18 % — not cure it. Hot-idle returns after the change are calmer (65/66/26 rpm under vs 193). No evidence it hurt.
- **Start parameters, this log only (one cold start, no hot restart, post-TB-clean):** `crankingThreshold` 500 fired on a cranking pulse (586) before the engine caught, so the exit didn't mean 'started'. The engine is caught from 685 → 1085 rpm (85.24–85.40). Will set the threshold to 750 on 2026-09-29: above the 586 pre-catch max and below the 832 lowest running dip. The afterstart delay went to 4, because this start crosses the afterstart target 0.39 s after a 750 exit (+47 rpm at ACTIVE vs −171 at 3). 1025 was rejected because hot idle dips below it and re-entry below the threshold is unverified. `engineStallRevs` (a direct RPM, max 250) must sit below cranking speed (158–222 rpm here); Will lowered it below 60 so fuel starts sooner. Companion change: the cranking fuel tail must match the running dose at exit (≈ +41 % at CLT 26 = ASE runtime-0; today's tail gives ~25–35 % less), or ASE comes down to meet it. Lock ≈ delay (8), fast restore, lock angle 24° stays. Cranking air vs this log's needs: 33 bin ~78–80 (now 63), 67 ~55 (now 37.5), 96 keep 30 (the unsaturated-oil hot restart needs ~30; heat-soaked needs ~15–20, and a CLT-only table can't tell them apart. Oil pressure can: 3.8–4.1 vs 1.8–2.1 bar, but it reads ~0 for the first ~1.5–2 s). Leave the afterstart RPM table until the next cold start.
- **Hot idle is 7–13 % rich (λ 0.78–0.85 vs 0.90), and so is the whole map (−6 % steady cruise).** The trim can't help: floor at −8 %, ~0.1 %/s, and it switches itself off below λ ≈ 0.795.
- **It has not always run rich — it tracks IAT.** Hot idle was on target at IAT 48 (08-24, 09-19) and +6 % lean at IAT 60; today at IAT 30 it is −11 % (cruise −6 %). ≈ 0.5 %/K, and within the 09-19 log 0.3 %/K at idle. That fits the ECU's dose following modelled charge temp while real idle air doesn't — but explains only ~4 of the 11 points at idle, and EGT (+40 °C) disagrees on direction. Mechanism unsettled.
- **The CLT/IAT rewire worked:** CLT voltage noise 1.58 → 0.12 quanta, p99.9 0.177 → 0.020 V, no spikes. Oil-pressure jitter is unchanged.
