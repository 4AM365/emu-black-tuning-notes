# Throttle-body cleaning — 2026-09-19 (the wandering servo was carbon)

> Digest of [tb_clean_2026-09-19.md](../tb_clean_2026-09-19.md) — the dense note is canonical; if they disagree, it wins.

**What this covers:** `fullchannels.csv` (= `after1720.csv` with all 564 channels; its first 602 s is the morning `whencold` recording) — a dirty-TB cold start and stall, the cleaning between morning and afternoon, a DBW relearn plus engine-off duty sweep, 15 minutes of clean hot idle on the re-cut 1.5–6.5 window, and a derived starting point for the Active / Armed tables.

**The rules:**

- **Carbon was the whole story of the "wandering" servo.** Dirty: plate stuck above its command for 20 % of idle-owned time with `DBW Out. DC` pinned at −40, two pedal saves from 536 rpm, one stall. Clean: 1.5 %, longest 3 s, zero duty-pinned episodes, no saves, no stall. The stall itself: at 7 % TPS the fouled plate passed half the air (MAP 22–24 kPa, MAP×RPM 18–19 k vs 37–38 k normal) — grime restricting flow, as Will read it.
- **The engine's air demand did not change and is proportional to RPM.** MAP×RPM/N = 34–37 kPa in every settled cell, dirty or clean, CLT 30–105, 1025–1500 rpm; cold oil adds ~5 %. Only the position moved: cleaning removed ≈ 2.0 % TPS of opening at the hot 1025 cell, ≈ 1.3 at 4 % TPS, ≈ 1.1 at 4.7 % (deposits cost most near closed). On top of that the **DBW relearn shifted the TPS frame by −1.09 %** (closed stop learned at 0.720 V vs 0.680 V; `TPS_new ≈ TPS_old − 1.09`).
- **The oil/TB clock is unchanged:** clean hot sustaining position 2.8 % TPS at 3 bar → 2.2 at 1.5 bar (≈ 0.4 % TPS/bar = 8 airflow-%/bar), all on the integrator because `idleActiveAirflow` is flat above 96 °C. The PID can carry it now.
- **Engine-off duty sweep (clean):** rest 6.3–6.6 % TPS at zero duty; break-away open +25…+28; **break-away closed −28…−31**, then the plate snaps to the 0.0 stop. No stable positions between 0 and ~6 % without the position loop — every idle position lives in that zone, so the servo idles at break-away force (holding duty −25…−33). `dbwMinDC` needs margin over ~−31: −40 was marginal clean and inadequate dirty; −100 removes it as a failure mode.
- **Clean-TB flow is proportional to position** (0 % is now the real closed stop; 11–12 k MAP×RPM per % TPS unsoaked, 14.6 soaked). The dirty TB had a ~3.5 %-TPS dead offset, which is why its tables could be flat in RPM. **The clean tables must fan out with RPM**: `TPS(N, CLT) = 2.9 × N/1025 × f(CLT)`, f = 1.00 / 1.06 / 1.13 (measured, CLT 96 / 75 / 60), 1.19 / 1.30 (converted from dirty data, CLT 45 / 30), 1.42 / 1.55 (extrapolated, CLT 15 / 0). In the 1.5–6.5 window that gives a 1000 row of 57.5/50.5/43.5/37.5/34/30/26.5/26.5 %, a 1250 row of 79.5/70.5/62/54/50/45/40.5/40.5, a 1500 row of 100/90.5/80.5/71/66/60/55/55 (CLT 0 → 105). Versus the 19:05 export: 1000 row right, 1250 row +8, 1500 row +18…+40 — i.e. the warm-up diagonal is low, which is what the warm-up PIDs said.
- **Hot column is a one-scalar choice between two measured oil states:** soaked (1025 → 18.5 %, 1225 → 25.5–29 %) gives 17.5 / 28 / 41 / 53 / 65 for 1000…2000; early-hot (first minutes, 1025 → 28 %) gives 26.5 / 40.5 / 55 / 69 / 83. Same RPM rule either way; the export mixes the two anchors and then goes flat.
- **The fouling history was a September step, not a steady climb** (`fouling_history.png`, one TPS frame): April 2.0–2.6, summer ≈ 2.6–3.3 (1000-equivalent), late August 2.6–3.4, then 4.1 on 09-09 and 3.9/4.8 on 09-19 → clean 2.5. Cleaning bought −2.3 % TPS at 1025 rpm, −1.3 at 1300, −1.1 at 1460 for the same air (`flow_characteristic_fouled_vs_clean.png`).
- **Armed table:** the current one already equals `S_hot(1025) + 0.25 × (S_hot(N) − S_hot(1025))` below 2000 rpm and validated 17 clean entries; the rule adds +2.5…+6 above 2300. Keep it, anchored to the hot base.
- **Window 1.5–6.5:** floor binds nothing (PID clamp binds first at 1.65); ceiling is adequate for the `idleRPM` curve but ~1–1.5 % TPS short for a cold start below ~15 °C or a cold rolling idle-up (1750/2000 rows clip). `idleAboveVSSTargetIncrease` re-enabled 200 / 5 km/h; `idleRAMPDownDecayRate` 200; `idleTargetBins` 1000/1250/1500/1750/2000.

**Key numbers:** hot sustaining TPS 5.9 dirty (old frame) → 2.9 early-hot / 2.4 soaked clean (new frame). Frame shift −1.09. Plate-stuck time 20 % → 1.5 %. Break-away closed −28…−31 % duty; rest 6.3–6.6 % TPS. Dirty→clean conversion `TPS_new = (TPS_old − 4.29)/0.55`.

**When to care:** any idle position, duty or clamp number recorded before 2026-09-19 17:20 describes a fouled throttle body in the old TPS frame. The fouling gauge from now on is the hot sustaining TPS at CLT 96 / 1025 / 18° / ~2.2 bar (2.4–2.5 today). Next: a clean cold start (decides the CLT 0–45 cells) and a 30–45 min hot soak.
