# Rolling return-to-idle wobble

> Digest of [idle_drive_wobble.md](../idle_drive_wobble.md) — the dense note is canonical; if they disagree, it wins.

**What this covers:** why RPM craters (~1800 → 525 at a 1200 target) and limit-cycles when you neutral-coast down to idle, and the three measured faults behind it.

**The rules:**

- **Fault 1 — rich low-RPM VE.** Lambda dove to 0.68 vs the 0.93 target as RPM cratered into MAP 60+ kPa. The 500-rpm VE row is the live lever; idle airflow rows below the 1200 target floor are never read.
- The −17–19% rich figure came from pre-correction E25 logs. After the −18% pump / +10% ethanol correction, the residual depends on fuel: still ~−18% at E60, only ~−9% at E25. **Re-measure lambda at the fuel you actually run before leaning the 500/842 rows** — a lean sized from the E25 log over-leans at E25.
- Short-term trim is clamped ±2–3% and slow: it can't correct a fast dip and won't show true VE. Set VE from measured lambda error, apply ~80%, lean both VE tables by the same ratio.
- **Fault 2 — fan correction drops out at speed.** The +13% `idleCoolantFanCorr` is VSS-gated off above ~56 km/h, so high-speed returns land on bare ~26.5% airflow with the PID clamped at +12. Fix with more PID authority; leave the fan +13% alone.
- **⚠ `idlePIDUpdateInterval` is not a lever — it isn't connected.** The symbol reads 200, but the loop **measurably runs ~40 ms**: in both July logs the PID output changes on ~54% of consecutive 25 Hz samples, with no spike at all at the 5-sample (200 ms) mark. A real 5 Hz loop would show an unmistakable staircase. It's a v2 relic or live-but-unexposed — never documented in any of the 23 v3 help pages, absent from the Airflow PID dialog, frozen at 200 across all 14 tune exports, and orphaned in the XML among starter/buzzer/CAN settings. **There is no 200 ms sampling lag; the only real delay is the ~160 ms DBW actuator lag.**
- **Fault 3 — idle locked out by the MAP gate.** `idleMinMapToActivate` at 25 kPa doesn't let idle grab until ~582 rpm on a hot coast-down — too late. Recommended **18 kPa** (valid window 17–32): idle catches at ~1060 rpm, inside the ~160 ms DBW lag. Re-verify coast-downs after the change.
- Feed-forward was oversized: PID sat saturated at −9.75%. Halving the corrections restored authority both directions.
- **2026-07-17 — the gate fix worked, and it exposed Fault 2.** The tune now runs `idleMinMapToActivate` = 18, so idle no longer locks out (stays active/state 2 down to ~324 rpm). But the airflow PID's output clamp was still 15, so idle **saturated and still couldn't add air**: two July logs show a clean saturated stall (RPM 1180→0, PID pegged +15, throttle only cracking to ~5.3%) and a ~1.2 s RPM limit-cycle (330↔2000) when a throttle blip rescues it. **Fix applied same day: `idleAirPIDOutMax` 15 → 25**, moving the PID ceiling from +0.84% to +1.40% throttle. Re-log a hot return-to-idle to confirm it no longer pins at the clamp.
- The **DBW-out-on-the-−35-rail** the driver saw is the servo yanking the plate shut *after* each rescue blip — a symptom of the hunt, not the cause; during the clean death DC sat ~0. Idle throttle authority window is 2.4–8.0% (`idleDBWTargetMin/Max` 24/80); the death topped ~5.3%, so PID authority, not the 8% ceiling, was the limit.
- **⚠ The gain rescale for the widened actuator range was never applied.** KP is still 2048 / KI still 205 (the proposed 1646/165 never reached the ECU), so loop gain runs ~1.24× hotter than intended — a candidate contributor to the hunting.
- **2026-07-30 — root cause = cold-oil friction.** Re-log `ahfuck less data.csv` (hot CLT, but oil still cold): with both prior fixes live it **still stalled** — RPM **1771→0 in ~1.3 s** — yet airflow PID reached only **+18 of 25** and idle ignition **+9° of ~+12°**, *neither saturated.* The engine was stumbling from oil-viscosity airflow starvation just before. Cold oil = several× more viscous → engine friction (FMEP) elevated (Heywood §13.3) → the thin idle torque margin is eaten → an uncatchable sag. That resolves the paradox: the **air lever had authority but is too slow** (~750 ms throttle lag) to answer a 1.3 s collapse, and the **ignition lever is fast but too small** (few % torque) to cover a big friction load. The state 2→4 hand-off dumping air 74→36.5% at 505 rpm was the last shove.
- **Windup is ruled out** (was the owner's first theory): PID output was −0.56% at re-engagement, never near the −4 floor, never saturated — out-torqued, not held down.
- **⚠ Correction:** the earlier "armed airflow too low, raise it" was **backwards.** `idleArmedAirFlow` decel bins = **40.5% vs ~35.7% warm need** = an intentional **+5% cold-oil margin** — a *margin, not a starve.* **Do not lower it,** and it can't be scheduled away (`oilTempInput=0`, no oil-temp sensor).
- **Fix — oil-temp idle-air modifier (owner's idea): physically correct.** Needs an **added oil-temp sensor** (EMU won't model it). Payoff: replace the *permanent* +5% margin with air only when oil is cold → *less* hot-idle variation + kills the windup concern. OEMs don't use oil temp directly — they proxy it with **coolant + post-start time decay** (Bosch p.235). A sensor-free **runtime-decay** idle-air add gets most of it for zero hardware; misses edge cases (short hot restart, cold soak).
- **Permanent design (2026-07-30):** armed table calibrated cold-oil-rich (hot-CLT/cold-oil pocket), oil-temp sensor **subtracts** air as oil warms. Only oil temp can see "hot coolant, cold oil" (CLT can't). **⚠ CRITICAL fail-safe catch:** the intent is "dead sensor → too-much-air," but the tune has `oilTempFailSafe = 100 °C` (hot) → a fault applies MAX subtraction → LEAST air → stall on the very failure being guarded against. **Set `oilTempFailSafe` cold (−40)** so a fault = zero subtraction = max air. Oil-*pressure* as the index is confounded by RPM (rises with pump speed) — use oil temp for the multi-RPM armed region; pressure only as a fixed-idle trim.
- **"DBW area-growth lag" = TWO effects on different clocks (not one).** (1) **TB bore expansion — ~20% of idle air, fast.** Cold-start ΔT≈70 °C opens the alu bore ~0.0115 mm radial faster than the stainless plate ≈ +2.6 mm² leak on a ~13 mm² idle area = ~20% (→~30% very cold). Real and significant, *not* small; settles with coolant/metal temp (minutes). This IS the "area growth." (2) **Oil-temp friction — large, slow tail** (10–20 min), the decay that outlives CLT. Split them by *when they stop*, not by size. (Earlier note wrongly minimized the TB effect to fit the oil story — corrected.) Bonus: idle DBW-opening vs runtime after CLT-hot is both the oil-warming signature and the shape of the runtime-decay table to build.
- Cranking (separate — the failed hot *restart*): owner cut hot-start air+fuel to stop flooding. Tune confirms `idleCrankingDC` = 86/63/31/**0%** by CLT — hot bin now 0% (no flare→sag/flood), cold keeps 86%. Verify a cold start still catches.
