## 3. The engine-off DBW routine (626–682 s) — duty→position curve, clean TB

This is the sweep [`airflow_actuator.md`](airflow_actuator.md) ("The measurement that would settle the floor") asked for.
Key-on, engine off, CLT 51, no airflow forces on the plate. Read the **slow ramps** (644–672 s); the fast stop-to-stop phase
at 628–638 s logs `DBW Out. DC` with the opposite sign to the plate motion (+80 → TPS 0.5, −100 → TPS 100) and is
the ECU's own routine, not a measurement to reuse.

| duty ramp | plate |
|---|---|
| 0 (rest) | **6.3–6.6 % TPS** (7.5–7.7 before the routine, 8.9 cold-dirty at t = 0, 10.9–12.5 hot-dirty at 497–602) |
| 0 → +25, slow | 6.6 → 7.2 — barely moves |
| **+25…+28** | **break-away open**: 7.5 → 18 % in one sample, then to 100 % by +31…+36 |
| 0 → −27, slow | 6.6 → 6.2 — barely moves |
| **−28…−31** | **break-away closed**: snaps to the hard stop, TPS 0.0 |
| −31 → −5 (relaxing) | stays at 0.0 |
| −5 → −3 | releases, jumps back to ≈ 6 |

So with the engine off there are **no stable positions between 0 and ~6 % TPS** — the limp-home spring pushes the plate
back up to the rest position from anywhere below it, and the main spring pulls it down from anywhere above. Every idle
position this engine uses (2.1–2.9 % hot, 4.0–4.6 % during warm-up, higher cold) lies in that bistable zone and exists
only because the position loop is modulating around a break-away force of ≈ −28…−31 % duty. That is exactly the
running holding-duty cluster (−25…−33) in §2 — the servo idles at break-away by design of the throttle body.

Consequences:

- **`dbwMinDC` must exceed the closed break-away with margin.** Clean, engine off, 12.x V: −28…−31. Running, the servo
  routinely used −30…−37 and touched −74. Dirty, it needed more than −40 and did not get it (§2). −40 was marginal for
  a clean TB and inadequate for a fouled one; −100 (the export) removes the clamp as a failure mode. The 09-19 morning
  "−40 chokes it" event needed a stuck plate and a wound-down command as preconditions — a clean TB removes the first.
- **Motor force ∝ duty × Vbat still applies** (09-08 note): the −28…−31 figure was taken key-on engine-off at battery
  voltage; on the alternator the same duty is ~10 % more force. Cranking (~10 V) is the weak case and the cranking
  airflow rides this same mechanism.
- **The fast phase at 628–638 s was a DBW relearn (Will), and `TPS voltage` measures the frame shift exactly.** Fitting
  `TPS` on `TPS voltage` (TPS < 40 %): before, `TPS = 27.52·V − 18.72` (0 % at 0.680 V); after, `TPS = 27.67·V − 19.92`
  (0 % at 0.720 V); residual sd 0.15 both. The closed stop learned 0.040 V higher, so **the same voltage reads 1.09 % TPS
  lower after the relearn.** Old-frame ↔ new-frame: `TPS_new ≈ TPS_old − 1.09` over the idle range. Nothing in §1–§2
  depends on it; §5 and §8 use the conversion.

What the full channel set settles:

- **The engine's air demand is the same on both throttle bodies and is proportional to RPM.** MAP×RPM/N sits at 34–37 kPa
  in every row — dirty or clean, CLT 30 or 105, 1025 or 1500 rpm. At the overlapping cells the air matches to 1–3 %

- **Carbon cost more opening near closed than further open** (after removing the 1.09 relearn shift): ≈ **2.0 % TPS** at

## 6. Actuator control range — 1.5–6.5 reconsidered

What the log says about the new window:

- **Floor 1.5.** The hard stop is 0.0 (§3). The hot sustaining position reached 2.1–2.2 at 1.5–1.75 bar and was still

- **Ceiling 6.5.** The highest post-clean idle position seen was 4.7 (CLT 60, 1460 rpm). The derived table (§8) puts the

  `idleRPM` curve as it stands; short by ~1–1.5 % TPS for a cold start below ~15 °C or a cold rolling idle-up.** Cranking
  cold cell 86 % = 5.8 % TPS.
- **Resolution.** Width 5.0 → 0.05 % TPS per airflow-% (was 0.056 at 2.4–8.0, 0.06 at 2.0–8.0). `DBW target` is logged
  to 0.1, so two airflow-% per resolvable step either way; not a factor.
- **Where the idle positions sit mechanically** matters more than the window numbers: everything from 2 to 4.6 % is
  below the limp-home rest (6.3–6.6) in the bistable zone where the servo holds at break-away force. No `idleDBWTargetMin`
  moves that; a throttle body whose limp-home rest is *below* the idle band (the Bosch swap already on the list) is the

- **`dbwMinDC` −100**: keep it off the table as a failure mode. The −40 clamp was what turned carbon stiction into 20 %
  of idle time with the plate stuck open and, on the morning log, into a choke when it let go.

Nothing here is a recommended edit; Will enters tune changes. What would move: the 1500/1250-row cells per §8, the

## 8. Scaling the Active and Armed tables for the clean TB and the 1.5–6.5 window — starting point

**Method.** The old tables cannot be rescaled through the actuator-rescale skill alone: that preserves TPS across a window
change, and the TPS itself moved (relearn −1.09, carbon 1.1–2.0 and position-dependent). And the old tables were not
"right" — the morning log ran them with the PID at +9…+23. So the tables are **derived forward from the measured
requirement**, not transformed from the old cells:

1. **Hot anchor (measured, clean, early-hot = guarantee case):** 1025 rpm at CLT ≥ 96 needs **2.9 % TPS** (new frame).
2. **RPM shape (measured):** air demand ∝ N (MAP×RPM/N constant, table above) and clean-TB flow ∝ TPS (zero at the
   learned stop). So the sustaining position is **proportional to target RPM**: `S_hot(N) = 2.9 × N / 1025`. Direct
   check: 1225 rpm soaked measured 2.93 vs 1025 soaked 2.50 → ratio 1.17 against N-ratio 1.195. *This is the shape change
   that matters:* the dirty TB had a ~3.5 %-TPS dead offset, so its tables were offset-linear and flat in RPM; the clean
   one has none, so the rows must fan out with RPM.
3. **CLT multiplier f(CLT)** on `S_hot`, from the clean warm-up cells where they exist and the dirty→clean conversion
   (§5) where they do not:

   | CLT | 96–105 | 75 | 60 | 45 | 30 | 15 | 0 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | f | **1.00** | **1.06** | **1.13** | 1.19 | 1.30 | *1.42* | *1.55* |
   | basis | measured | measured (≤ 4.0 at 1300) | measured (4.65 at 1460) | dirty 7.07 → 5.05 converted; also ≈ +6° spark ⇒ +15 % air on the measured 51.7 k | dirty 7.37 → 5.60 converted; same cross-check | **extrapolated** (no data below CLT 30) | **extrapolated** |

4. `TPS(N, CLT) = 2.9 × N/1025 × f(CLT)`, then `air% = (TPS − 1.5) / 5.0 × 100`, clipped at 100.
