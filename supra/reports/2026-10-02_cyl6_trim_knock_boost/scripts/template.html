MKIV Supra · 2JZ-GTE · EMU Black log study · 2026-10-02

Did the per-cylinder trim cut knock peaks and idle misfire?

The trim went live on May 4 2026 between 17:25 and 17:38. Before = every sample without trim, Mar 1 → May 4 (about 15 hours of running). After = every sample with trim, May 4 → Sep 29 (about 10 hours). Data: 119 decoded LogAutosave binaries.

Each test states a hypothesis and a prediction, lists the assumptions it rests on and how each was challenged, then gives a verdict. Confidence intervals are 95 % and resample whole drives, so one bad drive can't carry a result.

Question 1 · Did knock sensor peaks reduce after the trim? At idle, yes. The trim isn't proven to be the cause.
  • Idle, cyl 6 peaks above 2× its normal: 192 → 23 events in about 4.3 h of idle on each side (rate × 0.12, CI 0.04–0.31). Same fuel, Apr 20 – May 10: 164 → 4.
  • Untrimmed cyl 1 fell by the same factor (× 0.12), and idle peaks mostly hit several channels at once, so the drop can't be tied to the trimmed cylinders.
  • Boost: can't tell (0.8 min of comparable data after). Cruise on the same fuel: no change (× 1.2, CI 0.7–2.0).
Question 2 · Did misfire at idle reduce after the trim? Not established.
  • Rich lambda blips bigger than 0.02 λ at idle: 8 events in 149 min before, 6 in 229 min after. Rate × 0.49, CI 0.13–1.9.
  • Too few events to separate a change from chance. An earlier count (−53 %, then −64 %) came from a method that made extra blips at the edges of each idle stretch.
  • Idle lambda target was 0.931 on both sides through Aug 21.

Does a baseline shift fake the result? No. In the shared cells, each cylinder's median and its noise spread (median absolute deviation) are the same before and after to within one 0.02 V step of the channel. Counting peaks against a pooled median, against each period's own median, or as multiples of that period's own noise spread gives the same answer at idle and cruise (table below).

Idle = idle PID active (Idle state 2), CLT ≥ 80 °C. Cruise = MAP 30–95 kPa, 1500–4000 rpm. Boost = MAP 105–175 kPa, 3500–6000 rpm. Coverage in shared cells, before / after: warm idle 4.4 h / 4.2 h, cruise 6.9 h / 3.1 h, boost 2.2 min / 0.8 min.

4 · Data and limits

Where the numbers come from

  • The .emublog3 autosaves were decoded directly (504-byte records, 25 Hz), with every channel used here matched exactly against two CSV exports (Apr 8 and May 30). 20 duplicate or empty autosaves were dropped. Each sample's period comes from its own logged Injector 6 trim and Ethanol content.
  • Fuel differs between the two groups. Before = pump fuel (Mar 1–29) then E57; after = E57 (May 4–10), E25, then E14 from Aug 22. Ethanol suppresses knock, so lower ethanol after the trim works against the knock result, not for it.
  • Other work happened over the same months (idle airflow, VE, lambda settings, ignition smoothing in June). The idle knock result has a built-in control (cyl 1); the misfire proxies don't.
  • 25 Hz logging can't see individual firings at idle (~55 per second), and the wideband smooths them further. A rich blip is the outline of a misfire, not a count of them.
  • Normal knock = median of the same cylinder in the same 250 rpm × 10 kPa cell during the same period; a cell counts only if both periods spent at least 8 s there.
  • Knock-voltage CoV in boost (emu-black-knock-cov method, MAP > 100 kPa): 24.1 → 21.5 %. Two knock counts appear in the CSV exports all year, both after the trim and both on cyl 1.
